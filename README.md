One sentence that makes the whole meeting evaporate. No preamble, no agenda, no "let's circle back" — just the line that ends the call before anyone has time to unmute.

> The client said yes at 9, Finance said yes at 10, and the only person who hasn't said yes is the one who called this meeting to ask if anyone's said yes.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib-only, deterministic) writes the entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. No network, no secrets, no dependencies — just the sentence.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```
